The services

  • 01Social Media ManagementWe run the account. The calendar, the designs, the captions, the scheduling, and the replies in the comments. A report at the end of each month on how it performed.
  • 02Brand & Visual IdentityLogo direction, colour, type, social templates, and guidelines your own team can work from. Enough for the brand to hold its shape on a post, a menu or a storefront.
  • 03Content & CopywritingVisuals, reels, stories, captions, ad copy, bios and scripts. The writer and the designer work side by side, so the line and the layout arrive at the same point.
  • 04Media Production NewShort-form ad video for social, filmed with a crew or generated with AI, whichever the brief calls for. Product stills and motion graphics as well. You receive the script, the edit, the captions and a cut sized for Meta, TikTok and YouTube. Samples on request.
