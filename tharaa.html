Tharaa
Precious Metals

A gold and silver app launching in the UAE, where a customer can start at one dirham. We built the launch identity and the first season of social, in العربية, English and Hindi.

ClientTharaa Precious Metals
MarketUnited Arab Emirates
ScopeLaunch system, social
Set12 pieces
Scroll
01 / The situation

Every gold app in the Gulf looks like a bank

Search the category and the same three pictures come back. A vault door. A ticker chart in green and red. A bar of bullion lit like evidence in a case file. The market had agreed that trust looks like an institution, and every new entrant was copying the agreement.

Tharaa was arriving with a promise none of those pictures could carry. You can start with one dirham. The design problem was to give that single dirham the weight of something worth owning, before anyone had heard the name.

ثراء … كل درهم له مكانه Every dirham has its place

So the launch image is one coin in a museum case, roped off, lit from above. The amount is small on purpose. The treatment is not.

02 / The system

Navy and gold, rendered rather than photographed

  • One navy ground across every piece, so a post is recognisable in the feed before the logo is read.
  • Gold is the subject of the frame. It carries the message and sits at the centre of it.
  • Everything is built in 3D, which lets a one-dirham coin be lit with the same care as a kilo bar.
  • Arabic and English are set together in the layout. Neither one is added afterwards.
03 / Three languages

The buyer in Dubai is not one person

Arabic and English carry the product across the whole calendar. Hindi carries the occasions, because a large share of the people buying gold in the Emirates are buying it for a date in their own calendar. Dhanteras is the day gold is bought. That post is written in the language it is celebrated in.

Occasion posts. Written for the day, not translated into it.

04 / Teaching before asking

Nobody buys 999.9 until somebody explains it

Close to half the first season is education. What the purity number means. What separates 24K from 22K and 21K. What a gram is worth this morning. A first-time buyer arrives with questions they are slightly embarrassed to ask, and the brand that answers them is the brand they open when they are ready.

Education posts. The questions a first-time buyer will not ask out loud.

05 / Then the ask

The app appears once the question is answered

Product posts sit late in the sequence, numbered so the series reads in order. By the time the buy screen is shown, the audience already knows what purity is, what a gram costs, and why one dirham is a real holding rather than a gesture.

Product posts, numbered so the series reads in order.
